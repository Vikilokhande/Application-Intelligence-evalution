# Green Infrastructure Support Scheme — Complete Policy Reference

## Scheme Code: GREEN-INFRA-SUPPORT
## Issuing Authority: Directorate of Environment and Climate Change
## Effective Date: 2026-01-01
## Version: 2.0

---

## Section 1: Programme Overview

The Green Infrastructure Support Scheme (GREEN-INFRA-SUPPORT) is a government grant programme administered by the Directorate of Environment and Climate Change. The programme provides financial assistance to eligible organizations undertaking environmentally beneficial infrastructure projects.

The scheme aims to support projects that:
- Improve urban and rural environmental quality
- Reduce carbon emissions and environmental degradation
- Enhance biodiversity and ecosystem services
- Promote sustainable development practices
- Build community resilience to climate change

---

## Section 2: Eligibility Criteria

### 2.1 Eligible Organization Types

Only the following organization types may apply under this scheme:

- **Municipality** — Urban local bodies, Gram Panchayats, and other statutory local bodies with formal government recognition
- **Registered NGO** — Non-governmental organizations holding valid registration under Societies Registration Act, Section 8 Companies, or equivalent
- **Academic Institution** — Universities, autonomous colleges, polytechnics, and research institutions recognized by UGC, AICTE, or equivalent
- **Government Agency** — State government departments, central government agencies, and statutory boards

Applications from the following will NOT be accepted:
- For-profit private companies
- Individual applicants
- Unregistered entities or trusts
- Partnership firms
- Proprietary concerns

### 2.2 Project Eligibility

Projects must:
- Be located within India
- Demonstrate a clear, measurable environmental benefit
- Have a defined implementation timeline
- Not be a continuation of a previously funded project without prior approval
- Not duplicate an existing funded project in the same location

## Section 3: Financial Limits and Budget Requirements

### 3.1 Maximum Project Cost

The **maximum allowable total project cost** is **₹50,00,000 (fifty lakh rupees)**.

Projects with a total project cost exceeding **₹50 lakh** are automatically ineligible under this scheme and will be rejected at the preliminary screening stage.

Applications with a project cost exceeding **INR 5000000** (five million rupees) will be flagged for senior review.

The maximum allowable total project cost is not to exceed fifty lakh rupees (Rs. 50,00,000).

### 3.2 Minimum Project Cost

Projects with a total project cost below **₹10,000 (ten thousand rupees)** are not eligible. Such applications will be considered incomplete.

### 3.3 Eligible Expenditure Categories

Grant funds may be used for:
- Civil construction and infrastructure works
- Equipment and machinery procurement
- Professional consultancy fees (not exceeding 10% of total project cost)
- Community engagement and awareness activities
- Monitoring and evaluation
- Capacity building

Grant funds may NOT be used for:
- Land purchase
- Recurring operational costs beyond project period
- Staff salaries for permanent employees
- Debt repayment
- Retrospective expenditure (before grant approval)

### 3.4 Budget Consistency Requirements

All submitted budget documents must be internally consistent. A discrepancy of more than **2%** of the lower stated value, or more than **₹5,000 (five thousand rupees)**, whichever is higher, between budget figures stated in different submitted documents, will be treated as a **budget inconsistency**.

Budget inconsistency requires mandatory clarification before any substantive review decision.

---

## Section 4: Project Duration

### 4.1 Maximum Project Duration

The **maximum eligible project duration** is **24 months** (two calendar years) from the approved project start date.

Projects spanning more than **24 months** are ineligible under this scheme unless a formal extension has been pre-approved in writing by the scheme administrator before the application is submitted.

Projects spanning more than 24 months will not qualify for funding.

The maximum eligible project duration is 24 months.

### 4.2 Minimum Project Duration

Projects of fewer than **1 month** are not eligible under this scheme.

### 4.3 Duration Verification

The project duration stated in the project proposal must be consistent with:
- The implementation timeline submitted
- The budget disbursement schedule
- The milestones listed

## Section 5: Required Documentation

### 5.1 Mandatory Documents

All applications must include the following documents to be considered complete:

| Document | Type Code | Description |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Application Form | APPLICATION_FORM | Completed official application form with applicant details, project summary, and signed declaration |
| Project Proposal | PROPOSAL | Detailed project proposal including objectives, methodology, implementation plan, expected outcomes, and environmental impact assessment |
| Project Budget | BUDGET | Itemized project budget with line items, unit rates, and supporting justification for each expenditure category |
| Registration Certificate | CERTIFICATE | Valid registration certificate (NGO registration, institutional accreditation, municipal charter, etc.) |

Applications missing any of the above mandatory documents will be routed for **clarification** before substantive review can proceed.

### 5.2 Optional Supporting Documents

The following additional documents strengthen an application but are not mandatory:
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)
- Letters of support from local bodies
- Previous project completion certificates
- Financial statements (last 3 years)
- Technical specifications
- Land use permissions

## Section 6: Environmental Eligibility and Categories

### 6.1 Eligible Project Categories

Projects must fall into one of the following approved environmental categories:

- **Urban Greening** — Parks, tree planting, green corridors, rooftop gardens, urban forests
- **Water Conservation** — Rainwater harvesting systems, check dams, pond restoration, wetland conservation
- **Waste Management** — Solid waste processing, recycling infrastructure, composting facilities, waste-to-energy
- **Clean Energy** — Solar panels, wind turbines, biomass energy, energy efficiency retrofits
- **Biodiversity Conservation** — Wildlife corridors, habitat restoration, native species planting
- **Sustainable Agriculture** — Organic farming support, soil conservation, agro-forestry
- **Pollution Remediation** — Air quality improvement, soil remediation, water pollution control
- **Climate Resilience** — Flood mitigation, disaster risk reduction infrastructure

### 6.2 Environmental Benefit Statement

Every application must include a clear statement of environmental benefit that:
- Quantifies the expected environmental impact (e.g., tonnes of CO2 reduced, litres of water harvested)
- Explains how the project contributes to national environmental goals
- Describes the monitoring methodology for measuring outcomes

## Section 7: Review Process and Routing Policy

### 7.1 AI-Assisted Processing

Applications are processed through an AI-assisted pipeline that:
1. Extracts and verifies submitted information
2. Validates against scheme rules
3. Scores applications on risk and quality dimensions
4. Generates a routing recommendation

The AI system provides decision support only. **All final decisions must be made by authorized human reviewers.**

### 7.2 Routing Thresholds

| Condition | Routing Decision | Reviewer |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Missing required documents | CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED | Normal Reviewer |
| AI confidence below 0.55 | MANUAL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED | Senior Reviewer |
| Risk score ≥ 70 | SENIOR_REVIEW | Senior Reviewer |
| Risk score ≥ 40 OR cross-document contradiction detected | EXPERT_REVIEW | Expert Reviewer |
| All thresholds clear | NORMAL_REVIEW | Normal Reviewer |

### 7.3 Review Timelines

- Normal review: 15 working days from assignment
- Expert review: 25 working days from assignment
- Senior review: 10 working days from assignment
- Clarification response: Applicant has 30 days to respond

## Section 8: Scoring Criteria

### 8.1 Quality Score (0–100)

Applications are scored on overall quality. Higher scores indicate better quality:

| Score Range | Quality Level |
|-------------|--------------|
| 80–100 | Excellent — all requirements met, strong proposal |
| 60–79 | Good — most requirements met, minor gaps |
| 40–59 | Fair — significant gaps or weaknesses |
| 0–39 | Poor — major deficiencies, likely rejection |

### 8.2 Risk Score (0–100)

A risk score reflects the likelihood of errors, inconsistencies, or compliance issues:

| Score Range | Risk Level |
|-------------|-----------|
| 0–39 | LOW_RISK — standard review appropriate |
| 40–69 | MEDIUM_RISK — expert review required |
| 70–100 | HIGH_RISK — senior review required |

### 8.3 Risk Factors

Risk score increases for:
- Budget inconsistencies between submitted documents
- Missing mandatory documents
- Extraction confidence below 50% (poor document quality)
- Claimed project cost above ₹1,00,00,000 (one crore rupees) — beyond scheme limits
- Duplicate application detection
- Certificate present but certificate number not extractable
- Organization type not in eligible list
- Project duration exceeding 24 months
- Contradictions between different submitted documents
- Low completeness of required fields

---

## Section 9: Suspicious Indicators and Fraud Prevention

Applications are automatically flagged for additional scrutiny if any of the following are detected:

1. **Budget inconsistency** — Cost figures differ between documents by more than 2% or ₹5,000
2. **Low extraction confidence** — Document quality is poor (below 50% confidence)
3. **Very high project cost** — Cost exceeds ₹1 crore (beyond normal scheme limits)
4. **Duplicate submission** — Same applicant name and project title as previous application
5. **Certificate anomaly** — Certificate document present but no certificate number extracted
6. **Organization mismatch** — Organization type does not match eligible categories
7. **Duration violation** — Project duration exceeds 24 months without pre-approved extension

- Severity: ERROR

- Required: true
- Severity: WARNING

## Section 11: Disqualification Conditions

Applications are immediately disqualified if:
- Total project cost exceeds ₹50 lakh
- Organization type is not in the eligible list
- The application is a duplicate of a funded project
- Supporting documents contain fraudulent information
- The project has already received funding from another government scheme for the same activity
